What We Do

Everything your rental needs, under one roof

From the first photo to the final checkout, we manage every detail of your short-term rental so it performs at its best — and you never have to lift a finger.

Listing Setup & Optimization

Professional listing creation across Airbnb and other platforms — compelling copy, styling guidance, and photography coordination that makes your property stand out.

Dynamic Pricing & Revenue

Nightly rates tuned to Barbados seasonality, local events, and demand — maximizing your occupancy and income all year round.

24/7 Guest Communication

Fast, friendly responses to every enquiry, booking, and midnight question — plus smooth check-ins and check-outs that earn five-star reviews.

About Us

A family-led team that treats your property like our own

INtouch Property Management Inc is a Barbados-based property management company specializing in Airbnb and short-term rentals. We combine warm island hospitality with professional systems — detailed checklists, responsive communication, and meticulous care at every turnover.

Based in St. Michael and serving property owners across the island, we believe hosting should feel effortless for owners and unforgettable for guests.
